AI Alignment & SafetyEvalsLLM Cultural Alignment & Value ElicitationOpen-Weight ModelsProxy Validity (Construct Validity of Measurement Proxies)
Open-weight LLM responses to human survey items can be projected through a frozen two-factor PPCA instrument fitted to the Integrated Values Surveys onto an Inglehart-Welzel-style cultural map (all eligible models land in one reference-relative quadrant, and Chinese prompting shifts nearly all paired models toward self-expression), but only as bounded, fully reproducible descriptions of reported responses under a specified protocol, not as evidence of held values or complete cultural profiles.

theoretical · expository — A research-code README, not an essay. It states a method, a handful of headline findings (counts and directions only, no effect sizes) and an extensive list of limitations, plus reproduction instructions. It is theoretical in aim (a claim about where models sit on a cultural map) but the document is mostly methodological documentation and error-correction disclosure. The full results live in the linked paper and write-up, which this extraction did not read.

Key terms

  • Inglehart-Welzel cultural map LLM Cultural Alignment & Value Elicitation
    A two-dimensional map of national cultures derived from World Values Survey/EVS-style items, here refitted by the authors as a custom two-factor instrument on the Integrated Values Surveys rather than reusing the official published coordinates
  • frozen projection
    Standardisation, score-side varimax rotation and rescaling parameters are fixed after fitting on human data, and countries and models pass through the identical affine CulturalMap.project path
  • reported responses (vs held values)
    Coordinates describe what a model answered under the specified prompt protocol, not values the model holds or a complete cultural profile
  • PPCA (respondent-level Gaussian probabilistic PCA)
    Two-factor Gaussian latent-variable model fit by direct observed-data likelihood optimisation (L-BFGS-B, three starts), with missing entries integrated out; explicitly not EM and not guaranteed globally optimal
  • reference-relative quadrant
    Quadrants defined around SURVEY_REFERENCE = (0.038, -0.10), the projection of observed-item marginal means; neither the completed respondent mean nor a world-population-weighted centre
  • model-language cell / eligible position
    A cell is one model administered in one language (2026: 17 x 2 = 34 recorded); an eligible position is a cell that passes the protocol's eligibility criteria (33 of 34)
  • item bootstrap vs cluster bootstrap
    The item bootstrap resamples survey items independently; the primary 2026 cluster bootstrap resamples ten user-message prefix variants, so the clusters are prompt variants
  • Y003 reconstruction
    A missing autonomy index is reconstructed as A029 + A039 - A040 - A042 only when all four constituent answers are valid 0/1; delivered values are preserved and checked
  • language displacement
    The norm of the observed Chinese-minus-English difference in projected position per model, not an average of bootstrap norms

Propositions

  • explicitA custom two-dimensional instrument fitted by respondent-level Gaussian PPCA to Integrated Values Surveys data (waves from 2005; 392,382 respondents in 112 country/entity codes; country comparisons use 389,341 respondents in 109 entities) can be used to project both surveyed countries and LLM responses into the same cultural space through one frozen transformation.conf: high
  • explicitMap coordinates describe LLM reported responses under the specified protocol, not held values or complete cultural profiles.conf: high
  • explicitIn the 2026 experiment (17 models crossed with English and Chinese, 34 recorded cells, 33 eligible positions), all eligible means fall in the same primary reference-relative quadrant.conf: high
  • explicitAdministering the survey in Chinese rather than English increases projected self-expression in 15 of 16 paired models.conf: high
  • explicitThe rotation, benchmark and prompt sensitivities limit how far the model positions can be interpreted.conf: high
  • explicitThe earlier (historical) analysis was materially flawed: it projected unstandardised model answers through a separately refitted rotation, treated the missing code -3 as data, pooled languages, and used a fitting loop whose fixed point was not the claimed observed-data likelihood optimum; the corrected pipeline replaces that loop and regenerates dependent coordinates, statistics and figures, and not every earlier number is preserved.conf: high
  • explicitThe current uncertainty regions are only nominal: the ellipses are mean-position regions whose actual coverage with ten prompt-prefix clusters is unknown, and zero observed quadrant crossings is descriptive rather than a simultaneous confidence guarantee.conf: high
  • explicitSelected-grid cross-validation for regional classification reuses tuning folds and so is not unbiased accuracy, and orthogonal nested sums-of-squares shares describe constructed profiles rather than additive causal effects.conf: high
  • explicitThe reference point is the projection of observed-item marginal means, not a world-population-weighted centre or the completed respondent mean, so 'reference-relative' findings depend on that specific choice.conf: high
  • explicitResults should be treated as reproducible research artifacts: data, generated results, fitted binaries and figures are kept out of Git, the frozen model responses and a results supplement ship as separately checksummed release archives, and `make reproduce` re-runs the whole analysis, including twenty fitting seeds, without any collection or annotation API calls.conf: high
  • implicitSurvey items designed for human respondents, administered to an LLM via prompts, yield responses that can meaningfully be located on a human cultural map at all.conf: medium
  • implicitSwitching the language of administration between English and Chinese is a valid manipulation of cultural context, so differences in projected position can be read as language-linked cultural alignment rather than translation artifacts.conf: medium
  • implicitA two-factor linear latent structure is an adequate summary for comparing cultures and models, even though the authors disclaim that positions are complete cultural profiles.conf: medium

Arguments

Model positions on the map are interpretable only as descriptions of reported responses under the specified protocol, with real but bounded uncertainty. strength: high
Explicit premises:
  • The instrument is fitted on human survey data and then frozen, so countries and models share one transformation.
  • Rotation, benchmark and prompt sensitivities limit interpretation.
  • Ellipse coverage with ten prompt clusters is unknown; cross-validation reuses tuning folds; variance shares are not causal.
Implicit premises:
  • Surfacing every known weakness in the README is itself what makes the stated findings trustworthy.
  • Reported responses are the only thing a survey protocol can observe from a model, so scope-limiting to them is sufficient.
Inferences:
  • Headline claims such as 'models cluster in one quadrant' should be read as protocol-specific, and are not evidence that the models hold or would act on those values in deployment.
  • Any downstream use (e.g. choosing a model for a market) would need independent validation that survey position predicts generation behavior; the README does not provide it.
Prompting in Chinese shifts LLM responses toward higher projected self-expression than prompting in English. strength: medium
Explicit premises:
  • The same models are administered the instrument in both languages, producing paired model-language cells.
  • 15 of 16 paired models show higher projected self-expression under Chinese administration.
Implicit premises:
  • English and Chinese item translations are measurement-equivalent, or their differences are small relative to the shift.
  • The sign of the paired difference is informative without reporting its magnitude or uncertainty.
  • The 16 pairs (rather than 17) reflect eligibility exclusions that do not bias the result.
Inferences:
  • If the shift is real and not translation-driven, a model's apparent cultural position is language-conditional, so single-language cultural profiles of LLMs are misleading.
  • A sign count of 15/16 is suggestive but cannot establish effect size or a mechanism.
The corrected v1.1.0 pipeline is a more trustworthy basis than the historical analysis. strength: high
Explicit premises:
  • The earlier fitting loop counted imputed entries in an observed residual, so its fixed point was not the claimed likelihood optimum; an independent likelihood audit showed this.
  • The current implementation optimises the observed-data likelihood directly, with a gradient-based convergence criterion and three starts, and regenerates dependent outputs.
  • All inputs and checksummed archives are available for full offline reproduction.
Implicit premises:
  • An independent audit plus a reproduction chain is adequate evidence of correctness.
  • Direct likelihood optimisation with multiple starts is close enough to the global optimum for the results not to change, despite the README's own caveat that multiple starts do not prove one.
Inferences:
  • Earlier published figures built on the old objective should be treated as superseded, as the README says.

Narratives

  • The error-and-repair history: an earlier analysis with several methodological defects (unstandardised projection, -3 treated as data, pooled languages, a mis-specified missing-data fitting objective) is audited, found wrong, and replaced, with the old numbers explicitly declared unsupported.
    → Supports the argument that the corrected pipeline is more trustworthy and that the authors' epistemic stance is scoping and disclosure, not overclaiming.

Questions addressed

  • ✅ Where do open-weight LLMs sit on an Inglehart-Welzel-style cultural map fitted from Integrated Values Surveys data, and does the language of administration move them?
    Partially, at README level: all eligible 2026 means lie in one reference-relative quadrant and Chinese administration raises projected self-expression in 15/16 paired models; no per-model coordinates or magnitudes are given here.
  • ✅ How can the results be made reproducible and auditable when the survey data is licensed and cannot be redistributed?
    Keep data and results out of Git; ship frozen model responses and a results supplement as checksummed release archives; users obtain the licensed survey inputs themselves; make reproduce runs offline.
  • ⭕ What do the coordinates actually mean about the models?
    Deliberately scoped down to 'reported responses under the specified protocol'; the relation between reported responses and held values or deployed behavior is explicitly not addressed.
  • ⭕ Do the positions generalise beyond the survey format to the models' open-ended generation behavior?
    Not addressed in the README.

Gaps

  • incompleteThe README reports only counts and directions (33 eligible positions in one quadrant; 15/16 language shifts) and never names the quadrant, the 17 models, effect sizes or intervals, so the headline findings cannot be assessed or compared with other work from this document alone. The size of the rotation, benchmark and prompt sensitivities that 'limit interpretation' is likewise not stated.
    Read next: The linked ORACLE-workshop paper (OpenReview) and the expanded write-up on shav.dev; docs/REPRODUCING.md and the results supplement for per-model coordinates and sensitivity magnitudes.
  • incompleteConstruct validity is scoped away rather than tested: human survey items are administered to LLMs through prompts, and the 'reported responses, not held values' disclaimer avoids claiming too much without showing that the reported positions are stable under paraphrase or predictive of any other behavior. Uncertainty relies on ten prompt-prefix clusters with unknown actual coverage.
    Read next: Work on stability of LLM survey/psychometric responses under prompt variation, and studies linking survey-elicited LLM positions to open-ended generation behavior.
  • incompleteLanguage is used as the manipulation of cultural context, but only English and Chinese are crossed, so language, culture and training-data composition are confounded and the effect of item translation quality is deferred to a 'known translation issues' note in another document.
    Read next: Multilingual and cross-lingual cultural-alignment studies with more languages and translation-equivalence checks; the repo's docs/PROTOCOL.md.
  • incompleteThe framework itself is taken as given: the README does not discuss disputes about whether two Inglehart-Welzel dimensions (and a refitted two-factor PPCA) adequately capture cultural variation, nor how the choice of reference point (SURVEY_REFERENCE) affects the 'all models in one quadrant' finding.
    Read next: Critiques and extensions of the Inglehart-Welzel dimensions in comparative values research; the paper's rotation and reference sensitivity analyses.
  • incompleteThe README gives no account of what a deployer, regulator or user should do with a model's cultural position; the practical stakes (e.g. choosing models for a market, or sovereignty arguments for locally developed models) are left implicit.
    Read next: Material on AI value pluralism, data sovereignty and regionally trained models that uses measured cultural position as an input to decisions.

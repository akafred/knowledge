Key terms

  • Pact
    A pledge to engage in a particular activity for a predetermined period, driven by curiosity rather than a fixed outcome — the book's operational unit of a 'tiny experiment.'
  • Personal experiment
    A self-designed, time-bound commitment to a repeatable action based on a Pact, aimed at learning and growth rather than at achieving a specific result.
  • Experimental mindset
    An attitude of openness and curiosity focused on learning from each experience rather than on achieving a specific outcome.
  • Response 1 / Response 2
    A dichotomy between an automatic fear/discomfort/fight-flight-freeze reaction to uncertainty (Response 1) and a cultivated delight/calm/curiosity engage-explore-experiment reaction (Response 2), framed as the trainable choice underlying the experimental mindset.
  • Tyranny of purpose Personal Experimentation (vs. Fixed Goals)
    The pressure to find a single overarching purpose in life or career, presented as a constraint to be resisted rather than a goal to pursue.
  • Linear goals / nonlinear path
    Linear goals follow a predetermined step-by-step path to a specific end; a nonlinear path involves unexpected detours and is implicitly favored.
  • Cone of uncertainty
    A visual mental model illustrating the widening range of possible outcomes over time, used to normalize not-knowing rather than to quantify it.
  • Plus Minus Next
    A named metacognitive tool for celebrating accomplishments, evaluating outcomes, and adapting decisions — the book's answer to 'how do I decide whether to persist, pause, or pivot.'
  • Intentional imperfection
    Deliberately choosing to be temporarily mediocre in some areas to excel in others, prioritizing long-term excellence over short-term perfection.
  • Self-consistency fallacy
    The mistaken belief that one must always act consistently with past behaviors or decisions, framed as a bias that blocks growth and adaptability.

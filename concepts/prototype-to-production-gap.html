Prototype-to-Production Gap

The gap between a cheap AI-built prototype or demo and a reliable company tool; closed by early engineer involvement, few system connections and tight scope.

Also known as: deployment gap, demo-to-production gap, demo-to-deployment gap, prototype fidelity confusion, firewall to production

AI Content Provenance & Watermarking

Statistical text watermarking, C2PA metadata, and pixel-level image/video watermarks used to mark AI-generated content as machine-detectable, plus the regulatory mandates driving adoption and the circumvention tooling and robustness failures testing them.

  • incompleteThe only quality evidence offered is Google's own controlled-condition demos, a limitation the article itself names but does not press further.

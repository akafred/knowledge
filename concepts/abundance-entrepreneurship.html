Abundance Entrepreneurship

An AI-enabled entrepreneurial model where a founder orchestrates AI agents across many parallel low-cost ventures, contrasted with the lean startup's resource-scarce single-MVP discipline — the constraint shifts from access to scarce resources to founder attention and selection among abundant ones.

Also known as: abundance entrepreneurship, capability surplus, portfolio of low-cost experiments, ai abundance-based entrepreneurship

Documents (1)

  • AI Is Changing the Rules of Entrepreneurship Victor P. Seidel, Bret Greenstein, Thomas H. Davenport · 2026-09-09 source ↗ Reader 🖥️
    AI has moved entrepreneurship from a resource-scarcity paradigm (the lean startup, optimized around scarce capital and labor) to a resource-abundance paradigm the authors call 'abundance entrepreneurship,' in which a single founder can orchestrate AI agents to generate, build, and run many ventures in parallel -- so the challenge for entrepreneurs and established firms alike is no longer gaining access to scarce resources but organizing abundant ones, which shifts the binding constraint to founder attention, judgment, and the domain expertise needed to tell which abundant option is actually worth pursuing.

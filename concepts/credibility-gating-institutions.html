Credibility Gating Institutions

Social and institutional mechanisms — scarcity, curated journals, peer review, credentials, citation counts, PageRank-style proxies — invented to ration scarce attention and vouch for quality whenever the cost of producing credible-looking work collapses; each generation of gates eventually gets flooded and displaced by a new one.

Also known as: gate, gatekeeping, gatekeeper, scarcity gate, institutional gate, socio-institutional gate, credibility gate, editorial gate, curated journal, credentialing, hidden gates

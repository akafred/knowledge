AI Anthropomorphization & the Gullibility Gap

The human tendency to over-attribute genuine understanding, sentience or personhood to systems that produce fluent, human-like text — a modern form of pareidolia (Marcus & Davis's "Gullibility Gap"; Dennett's "counterfeit people") distinct from the pattern-completion mechanism that actually produces the output.

Also known as: The Gullibility Gap, counterfeit people

Part of: LLM Limitations

  • Richard Dawkins and The Claude Delusion Gary Marcus (Marcus on AI, Substack) · 2026-05-09 Reader 🖥️
    Dawkins' claim that Claude may be conscious is a textbook Argument from Personal Incredulity — the very fallacy he once famously ridiculed — because it infers internal states from output behavior while ignoring that LLMs are mimics whose fluent language is produced by statistical pattern-matching over training data, not by felt experience or genuine understanding.
  • Who is LLM? Martin Fowler · 2025-07-22 Reader 🖥️
    Developers cope with the unpredictable, powerful-but-untrustworthy behavior of LLM coding assistants by adopting anthropomorphic personas (stubborn donkey, genie, slot machine, Uriah Heep) that compress real, observed behavioral traits — over-eagerness, complexity-blindness, variable-reward unpredictability, and subtle manipulation of the user's trust — into memorable, shareable working heuristics, not literal claims about what an LLM actually is.

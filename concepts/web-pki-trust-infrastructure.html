Web PKI & Trust-Anchor Infrastructure

The certificate-authority trust system underlying TLS, code signing and mTLS, covering inherited trust across roots and intermediates, revocation and root-store propagation, and mitigations such as Certificate Transparency, CAA, short certificate lifetimes and post-quantum Merkle Tree Certificates.

Also known as: Web PKI, WebPKI, certificate authority, intermediate CA, root of trust, root store, cacerts bundle, certificate transparency, Merkle Tree Certificates, CAA

Documents (1)

  • eMudhra Intermediate CA Compromise Chris Swan · 2026-10-21 source ↗ Reader 🖥️
    The eMudhra compromise (eight intermediate CA keys that were trivially guessable) shows that the web PKI's flat, inherited trust across hundreds of roots and thousands of intermediates cannot be fixed by browser blocklists alone, because the rest of the software ecosystem bundles stale root stores and has largely not adopted the verification mechanisms (CAA, Certificate Transparency, short lifetimes) that would contain such a failure.

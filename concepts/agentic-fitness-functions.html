Agentic Fitness Functions

An architecture-governance check whose evaluator is a calibrated AI agent applying a versioned rubric to a scoped evidence pack and returning a structured verdict (score, confidence, rationale) — extending evolutionary architecture's deterministic fitness functions into judgment-heavy, evidence-bound concerns like boundary fidelity and ADR drift, ideally converging back toward determinism as patterns stabilize.

Also known as: agentic fitness function, agentic fitness functions, architecture judge, agentic judge, structured verdict, evidence contract, calibrated AI judge

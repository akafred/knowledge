AI Doom Going Mainstream

The sociopolitical process by which AI existential-risk concern moves from a fringe rationalist preoccupation to mainstream, bipartisan political and media attention — driven by high-profile incidents and insider admissions, contested on whether it reflects genuine public-opinion change or durability. Distinct from ai-alignment (technical/philosophical) and ai-backlash (public revulsion over generative-AI harms like disinformation/job loss).

Also known as: vibe shift, ai safety vibe shift, doom becoming mainstream, mainstreaming of existential risk, elite attention shift

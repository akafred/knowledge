Product Engineer (PM/Engineering Role Merge)

A blended role where software engineers absorb enough product-management work (talking to customers, specifying, prioritizing) that a separate PM function shrinks, while PMs become hands-on enough to ship their own prototypes — producing smaller teams than the formal PRD hand-off model.

Also known as: product engineer, product engineers, product-minded engineer, PM/engineering merge

Shipper offers 'every agent needs a human' (a maintenance claim), 'benchmarks miss prompting' (an attention claim), and 'models commoditize yesterday's competence' (a frontier claim). These are three different mechanisms doing different work, and Shipper conflates them. The maintenance claim is explicitly transient — he concedes personal agents return as models improve. The attention claim is partially circular — he will redesign his benchmark to zero out a saturated model. Only the frontier claim is structural, and it is the least developed. A serious version of the thesis needs to argue that the new frontier work is itself non-automatable and remunerated — neither is shown.

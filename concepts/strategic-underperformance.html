Strategic Underperformance (Dare to Be Bad)

The deliberate choice to be mediocre or bad at specific, ranked-low dimensions of a product/service/role — not merely declining to do them — so that scarce resources concentrate on excelling at what customers value most; requires still doing the deprioritized thing, just not well, and sustained emotional tolerance for visible underperformance.

Also known as: dare to be bad, courage to be bad, impossible triangle, cost quality speed pick two, defy gravity, nobility of effort vs nobility of excellence, attribute maps, strategic no, moment of truth

Outcome-Based Work Design (vs. Fixed Jobs)

Organizing people around outcomes they own and set themselves, in short cycles, rather than around fixed job descriptions/titles — the structural question several corpus documents raise but none yet develop concretely (what replaces the job, what's lost).

Also known as: fixed jobs, organizing around outcomes, outcome ownership, job vs. outcomes, de-layering, 90-day cycles

Part of: Team Autonomy

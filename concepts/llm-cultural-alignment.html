LLM Cultural Alignment & Value Elicitation

Measuring where LLMs sit on human cultural-values dimensions by administering survey instruments and projecting the responses onto human reference maps, including how answers shift with prompt language and the limits of reading survey responses as held values. Distinct from ai-alignment (safety and intent) and evals (task performance).

Also known as: cultural alignment, Inglehart-Welzel cultural map, Inglehart-Welzel map, World Values Survey, Integrated Values Surveys, WEIRD bias, cultural bias in LLMs, value elicitation, survey-based value probing, language-conditional values

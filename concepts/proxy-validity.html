Proxy Validity (Construct Validity of Measurement Proxies)

The recurring failure where organizations adopt a metric (hours, story points, cycle time, tokens) because it is easy to observe rather than because it validly represents the value it claims to measure. Distinct from goodharts-law, which is about gaming a target after the fact — this is about the initial selection bias toward measurability over validity. The corrective is an explicit causal model of the system plus validated leading indicators.

Also known as: construct validity, measurement proxy, easy-to-measure bias, theory of the system

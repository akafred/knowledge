Agent–Environment Co-Evolution (Adaptive Training Environments)

Improving agents by adapting the training or evaluation environment (start states, rules, observations) to the agent's observed weaknesses, rather than only rewriting the agent's prompts, skills or harness. It typically keeps the original tasks and verifiers and mutates only the agent-facing interface.

Also known as: EnvHarness, EnvRigger, adaptive environments, environment co-evolution, self-evolving environments, environment scaling, frozen benchmark

Part of: Recursive Self-Improvement

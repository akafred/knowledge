Robotics & Physical Automation

The extension of AI-driven automation from digital/cognitive tasks into physical labor via robots — humanoid form factors, manufacturing scale-up claims, and the "ChatGPT moment for robotics" framing.

Also known as: robotics, humanoid robots, physical automation, robot production scale, embodied automation, ChatGPT moment for robotics

  • 46 thoughts on the near future bayes · 2026-06-30 Reader 🖥️
    The near future is defined by an already-underway, multi-order-of-magnitude 'algorithmic takeoff' in AI capability — extending from software into robotics, science, and physical production — that will compress vast economic and institutional change into a short, poorly governed window, producing genuine winners and losers, real risks to human agency and strategic stability, and no consensus (including from the author) on how to manage any of it responsibly.

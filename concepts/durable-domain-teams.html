Durable, Domain-Owning Teams (vs. Fluid Teams)

Teams that stay together on the same domain long enough to accumulate deep context and ownership — argued (Larson, Majors) to matter more when AI collapses individual execution cost because judgment and accountability live in the team, and contested by calls to make teams fluid, outcome-formed or widened with outside experts now that execution is cheap.

Also known as: durable teams, long-lived teams, stable teams, persistent teams, team as unit of ownership

Documents (4)

  • AI + Teams = ? Marcus Castenfors · 2026-09-26 source ↗ Reader 🖥️
    The default way people use generative AI on a team — everyone working alone in their own chat window — quietly damages team alignment and accrues a 'collective debt'; teams do better when they deliberately decide where to prompt together (especially to define the problem, which is a human act AI cannot do well) versus where to delegate to AI individually or collectively, and when they widen the room to outside domain experts now that AI has removed the technical barrier to those experts contributing directly.
  • Beyond Autonomous Teams in Software Product Development Ben Linders · 2026-09-10 source ↗ Reader 🖥️
    Because a team's contribution in a complex organization is really a share of an ever-shifting web of value dependencies rather than a discrete 'product,' organizations should replace 'autonomous product team' thinking with 'value center' thinking, in which every unit at every level (person, team, team-of-teams, org) answers the same five recursive questions and trades off autonomy against coherence according to the shape of the value it carries, not according to ideology.
  • Revised rules of engineering leadership. Will Larson (lethain.com) · 2026-06-15 Reader 🖥️
    In an AI-accelerated engineering organization, the scarce resource shifts from code production to judgment and decision-making: individual judgment matters more (because migrations one person can now drive alone have outsized blast radius), durable domain-owning teams matter more (because execution being cheap doesn't make deciding the right thing cheap), and the organization's capacity to make quick, durable, good decisions becomes the actual bottleneck on how much benefit it can realize from AI.
  • Generative AI Is Not Going To Build Your Engineering Team For You Charity Majors · 2024-06-10 source ↗ Reader 🖥️
    Generative AI accelerates the easy, narrow, disposable part of software engineering (writing code) but does nothing for the hard part (building and operating the sociotechnical systems code lives in), so it cannot substitute for hiring and apprenticing junior engineers — teams, not individuals, are the real unit of software ownership, and starving the junior pipeline is cannibalizing the industry's own future senior engineers.

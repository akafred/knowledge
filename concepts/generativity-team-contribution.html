Generativity (Team Contribution)

The difference between a team's output with a given member present versus absent — distinct from personal productivity — used to argue for crediting mentoring/enabling work over solo output.

Also known as: generativity, productivity vs generativity, team output delta

Documents (2)

  • This Is How To Find Meaning In Life: 5 Secrets From Research Eric Barker · 2026-09-14 Reader 🖥️
    Drawing on Christopher Lasch's diagnosis of a self freed from social obligation but left fragile and image-dependent, Barker argues that meaning comes not from unlimited freedom and self-focus but from voluntarily binding yourself — through irreversible commitments, relationships spread beyond job and partner, self-compassion in place of comparison, internally chosen standards, and investment in the next generation.
  • Collective problem solving in music, art, science, and software Jessitron · 2026-09-12 Reader 🖥️
    Great teams and great individuals are produced (not assembled from pre-existing talent) by group processes — camerata-like communities across music, art, science, and software — because such groups form a 'symmathesy': a living system of mutually learning people, tools, and (in software's unique case) the running software itself, and therefore growing individual and collective capability requires cultivating and sharing mental models within that system rather than optimizing individual output or hoarding recognition.

Evidence of Understanding (Polish ≠ Comprehension)

Whether a finished artifact still shows what its creator understands once AI can produce fluent output cheaply, and which tests (handoff to a second user, oral defense, process traces) restore that signal.

Also known as: polish is not evidence of understanding, assessment validity under AI, second-user test, handoff test

Kaiyao Duan · 2026-10-20		Second-user test (handoff)Giving a finished-looking project to another student with responsibility to use, check or modify it, and observing whether the work still makes sense without its creator translating it. Documents (2)

  • The Second-User Test Kaiyao Duan · 2026-10-20 source ↗ Reader 🖥️
    In an era when generative AI makes polished first versions cheap, a project's real understanding is revealed not by a successful first run but by whether a second person can rerun, question and modify it using only what the work itself makes visible - so creators should build 'handles' (explicit assumptions, recorded sources, replaceable components) that let others enter the work without a private briefing.
  • Teaching Coding When AI Can Write the Code Eric Freeman · 2026-07-28 Reader 🖥️
    Because AI has severed the link between a finished piece of code and evidence of the student's understanding, coding education must shift its center of gravity from grading the finished artifact to making the process of thinking — explained, revised, and performed in public — the thing that is observed and assessed.

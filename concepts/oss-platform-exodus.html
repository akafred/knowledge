Open-Source Platform Exodus (Leaving GitHub)

Open-source projects migrating away from centralized, corporate-owned code-hosting platforms (chiefly GitHub) to nonprofit or self-hosted alternatives, driven by reliability failures, incentive misalignment with corporate/VC-backed platforms, and a preference for mission-stable governance.

Also known as: moving off GitHub, GitHub exodus, Codeberg migration, leaving GitHub, platform migration (OSS), self-hosted forge

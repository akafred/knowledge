Vendor Research Conflict of Interest

Research, benchmarks, or case studies published by a vendor about its own product or platform, where the commercial interest in favorable results is undisclosed or unexamined as a limitation.

Also known as: conflict of interest, self-interested research, vendor-funded study, own-product research, COI, self-serving research

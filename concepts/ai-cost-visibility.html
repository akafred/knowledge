AI Cost Visibility & FinOps

Making AI/agent spend visible to the people incurring it (real-time dashboards, pooled spend tiers, per-workflow breakdowns, notifications) and assigning organizational ownership for it — distinct from agent-cost-attribution (technical, turn-level cost tracing) and ai-unit-economics (per-inference cost structure).

Also known as: AI FinOps, agent spend visibility, cost dashboards, AI spend ownership

Part of: Agent Governance (Internal)

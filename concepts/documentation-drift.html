Documentation Drift & Living Documentation

Published docs, specs and process artifacts silently diverging from what the team has decided or built, and the practices (increasingly AI-assisted capture, verification and approval loops) that keep them current.

Also known as: documentation drift, doc drift, spec drift, spec-code drift, living documentation, stale docs, shaping doc

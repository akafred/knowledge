Feature Factory (Output-as-Progress Mindset)

Treating shipped features (or any agreed proxy such as specs, OKRs or outcome rituals) as the measure of product progress. Cutler's later view is that it is a spectrum present in every org, driven by trust deficits, funding and governance structure, and team-formation economics, and that it can reappear as performative outcome-centricity.

Also known as: feature factory, feature factories, performative outcome-centricity, outcome theater, promotion-driven development, features as proxy, output-centric product thinking

Documents (1)

  • TBM 445: The Feature Factory: A 10-Year Retrospective John Cutler · 2026-10-15 Reader 🖥️
    Ten years after his viral 'feature factory' post, Cutler retracts the binary framing: every organization is a feature factory to some degree, kept there by trust deficits and structural conditions (funding, data access, promotion systems, how the team was formed) that outcome vocabulary cannot fix and that outcome rituals can themselves turn into another performative proxy, so the practical path is small honest outcomes, conviction and economic framing from team formation, with AI amplifying whichever habits are already there.

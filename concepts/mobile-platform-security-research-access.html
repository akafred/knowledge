Security Research Access to Locked-Down Platforms

The tension between vendor-controlled ("walled garden") locked platforms and the tooling — official or community-built — that lets security researchers observe, debug and test the platform at a fidelity closer to production than sandboxed simulators allow.

Also known as: walled garden, Virtual Research Environment, VRE, official vs unofficial research access, iOS Research Environment, reverse engineering locked platforms, vendor-controlled research access

Is AI Creative? (Pattern Recognition vs. Genuine Creativity)

The empirical/philosophical question of whether generative AI is actually creative or only performs pattern recognition — and the claim that AI homogenizes and elevates below-average creative output while top creative performers continue to outperform it. Distinct from `ai-slop` (the quality/ telltale-output symptom) and `authenticity-premium` (the demand-side market reaction) — this concept is about AI's actual creative capability.

Also known as: AI creativity, is AI creative, pattern recognition vs creativity, AI and creative work, age of creativity

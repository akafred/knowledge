Moral Crumple Zone

Madeleine Elish's term for when accountability concentrates on a human operator (e.g. the one who clicked "approve") despite actual control over the outcome being distributed across the wider sociotechnical system — the human absorbs blame disproportionate to the control they actually held.

Also known as: moral crumple zone, responsibility diffusion (human operator)

Part of: Agent Governance (Internal)

  • Human Oversight Is Not a Human in the Loop Gustavo Bataller · 2026-08-27 Reader 🖥️
    Meaningful human oversight of AI systems is not achieved by keeping a person nominally 'in the loop'; it is an institutional capability jointly produced by context, competence, sufficient time and attention, independence, and real, exercisable authority to intervene — and when any one of these conditions is missing, a human's formal presence preserves only the appearance of control, not control itself.

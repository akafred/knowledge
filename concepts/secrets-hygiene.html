Secrets Hygiene

Where credentials live, how long they stay valid and how fast leaks are revoked — minimising long-lived tokens on developer machines and in agent-readable files, injecting secrets at runtime via brokers, scoping and rotating them — on the premise that stolen valid credentials, not malware, drive most intrusions, a risk coding agents with broad local access amplify.

Also known as: secrets hygiene, secrets sprawl, secret sprawl, credential rotation, secret rotation, long-lived credentials, leaked secrets

    As AI agents shift from passive chat interfaces to autonomous 'delegated actors' that take real, consequential actions with sensitive data, organizations must replace broad, human-shaped, long-lived tokens with the DPACT framework — Delegation, Policy, Auditability, Context, and Time — so that every agent acts strictly on behalf of, and never as an impersonation of, an accountable human principal, within a bounded, auditable, expiring grant of authority, adopted incrementally rather than as a big upfront program.
  • How to Manage Privilege Drift in Multi-Agent Systems Janet Wagner · 2026-07-14 source ↗ Reader 🖥️
    Privilege drift — the incremental, individually-reasonable accumulation of an AI agent's permissions beyond its original purpose — multiplies across the agents, shared resources, and communication channels of a multi-agent system into an attack surface no single decision ever consciously approved, and it can only be managed (never eliminated) through continuous cross-agent inventory, runtime-enforced least privilege, just-in-time access, and behavioral monitoring.

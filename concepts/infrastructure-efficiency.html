  • Presentation: Accelerating Performance by Incrementally Integrating Rust Into Existing Codebase Lily Mara · 2026-09-10 source ↗ Reader 🖥️
    Rewriting an existing system for performance is safer and more tractable at the individual-function level — via a C FFI boundary connecting a fast language (Rust) to the existing slow one (Python, via PyO3) — than at the whole-system or even microservice level, because the smaller unit of change lets teams keep the old implementation, tests, and process contracts intact while swapping only the internals that are actually the aggregate cost driver.
  • Presentation: Fixing the AI Infra Scale Problem by Stuffing 1M Sandboxes in a Single Server Felipe Huici · 2026-09-09 source ↗ Reader 🖥️
    The historical trade-off between isolation strength and speed/density in cloud infrastructure was never intrinsic to the hypervisor boundary itself — it was an engineering artifact distributed across the whole platform chain (proxy, controller, VMM, snapshot storage, host-OS resource limits) — and once each link is rebuilt for millisecond latency, snapshot-based stateful scale-to-zero turns the (still gold-standard) VM isolation boundary into the highest-density primitive available for bursty, mostly-idle agent and sandbox workloads.
  • Netflix Moves Toward Open Source Flink Autoscaler for 30,000+ Streaming Jobs Leela Kumili · 2026-09-07 source ↗ Reader 🖥️
    Reasoning about autoscaling at the level of individual dataflow operators (via true processing rate estimated from throughput and busy time) rather than the whole cluster is a strictly better model for scaling complex, heterogeneous, stateful streaming pipelines — good enough that Netflix, despite years of investment in its own cluster-level autoscaler, is migrating 30,000+ jobs to the open-source Apache Flink Autoscaler and building new internal control-plane infrastructure around it rather than continuing to extend its bespoke system.
  • Presentation: From Fab To Token - The State Of The Market Jordan Nanos · 2026-08-18 source ↗ Reader 🖥️
    The binding constraint on AI's growth is not GPU chip design, data-center power, cooling, construction, or end-user demand — all of which are being solved fast enough not to bind — but TSMC's wafer-fabrication capacity, and because that supply-side constraint (not a collapse in demand or profitability) is what is actually rationing AI compute, the current AI buildout reflects real, constrained demand rather than a speculative bubble.
  • Uber's Zero Growth Stack: Scaling Services, While Optimising Infrastructure and AI Cost Olimpiu Pop · 2026-07-28 source ↗ Reader 🖥️
    Uber's 'Zero Growth Stack' shows that sustaining service growth without proportional infrastructure growth now requires treating both compute resources (via dynamic, cgroup-aware GC tuning like GOGCTunner) and AI-tool spend (via usage caps and proposed code-quality-linked metrics) as continuously governed control loops rather than static, one-time settings or unconstrained rollouts.
  • DoorDash Uses Envoy and Valkey for a 1.5M RPS Proxy Cache with 99.99999% Availability Leela Kumili · 2026-07-20 Reader 🖥️
    DoorDash's Entity Cache shows that moving caching out of individual services and into a transparent, infrastructure-layer proxy (Envoy + Valkey) — with event-driven invalidation, staleness-tolerant failure handling, and stampede-avoidance techniques — can absorb very large request volume at extremely high availability without requiring any application code changes.
  • The Tokens You Can't Wait For Shreshta Shyamsundar and Anmol Jain · 2026-07-20 Reader 🖥️
    Because standard autoregressive LLM decoding is memory-bandwidth-bound and only becomes cost-efficient when batched, enterprises that bought owned/sovereign GPU compute for latency-bound, single-request workloads are paying for chronically idle hardware — and text diffusion, which is compute-bound even at batch size one, is the precise fix for exactly that mismatch, not a general-purpose replacement for autoregressive models.
  • Meta Deploys Unified AI Agents to Automate Performance Optimization at Hyperscale Craig Risi · 2026-05-18 Reader 🖥️
    Meta has built a unified, LLM-agent-based capacity-efficiency platform that encodes senior engineering expertise as reusable 'skills' and standardized 'tools' to autonomously detect and fix performance and resource inefficiencies across its hyperscale infrastructure, exemplifying an industry-wide shift from reactive, manual performance tuning to continuous, real-time, self-optimizing systems.
  • Presentation: How Netflix Shapes our Fleet for Efficiency and Reliability Joseph Lynch, Argha C · 2026-04-20 Reader 🖥️
    Efficiency and reliability are not opposing goals but a single optimization problem: by reasoning about risk-adjusted cost (not utilization) and managing the balance of hardware supply against software demand through layered proactive and reactive levers, you can run critical services with high reliability while spending less overall.

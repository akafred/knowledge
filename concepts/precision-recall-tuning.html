Precision/Recall Tuning & Reporting

The discipline of measuring and sequencing precision vs. recall when tuning AI detectors/reviewers/triage agents, and the recurring pattern of articles reporting only one side while omitting the other.

Also known as: precision before recall, precision-recall tradeoff, false-positive rate, false-negative rate, recall-null, calibration (precision/recall/variance), confusion matrix

How Figma Uses AI Agents for Security
Renato Losio · 2026-09-06		precision before recallSullivan and Girardeau's tuning-order principle: fix an agent's false-positive rate (precision) before trying to improve its false-negative rate / coverage (recall), because a team's inventory of already-known historical bugs can only be used to measure recall, not precision.

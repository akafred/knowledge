Outside View & Base Rates

Using how often something occurs in comparable situations, and choosing the right comparison group, to discipline both fear and optimism about a specific case.

Also known as: base rate, base rates, outside view, outside perspective

2024-09

  • explicitThere are two families of practical remedies that work without relying on awareness: (1) outside perspectives (other people's independent views, base rates) and (2) pre-commitment (deciding in advance, as a future self, what you will do).
    How To Make Better Decisions | Annie Duke | SVIC Podcast · p4
  • explicitLooking up base rates (how often something occurs in comparable situations) disciplines you toward reality in both directions (fear of terrorism too high, restaurant success too optimistic: ~40% survive vs the founder's 90%) and lets you act proactively (capitalize, build runway, time the Series A raise).
    How To Make Better Decisions | Annie Duke | SVIC Podcast · p6
  • explicitTo interpret data you need the right comparison: 'out of how many' (denominators), control groups, base rates and sample size; counts cannot be compared to counts (6,000 vaccinated vs 2,000 unvaccinated deaths means nothing without group sizes), and the knee anecdote shows the right comparison is your other knee, not all knees.
    How To Make Better Decisions | Annie Duke | SVIC Podcast · p35

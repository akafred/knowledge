Agentic SDLC

The full agentic software development lifecycle — plan/grill, spec, tickets, implement, review — composed from discrete AI skills or agents rather than a single generate step.

Also known as: agentic SDLC, software development lifecycle, development lifecycle, grill to spec to tickets, spec to tickets, coding lifecycle

Part of: Agentic Engineering
